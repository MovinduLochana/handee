## Design Context

### Users
Sri Lankan homeowners and independent tradespeople (plumbers, electricians, AC technicians, painters). Homeowners are looking for trusted professionals for emergency fixes and routine maintenance. Providers need a reliable platform to get jobs and build their reputation. 
**Core Emotion**: The interface must evoke **Trust and Confidence** for both parties.

### Brand Personality
**3-word personality:** Reliable, Fast, Trustworthy.
**Emotional goals:** Customers should feel peace of mind that they are hiring strictly verified professionals. Providers should feel assured by a fair, structured dispatch and payout system. The voice and tone should be highly professional, structured, and clear.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Visual tone:** Minimal & Clean. 
The UI should be completely uncluttered to highlight essential information like verification badges, AI-estimated pricing, availability slots, and ratings. 
**Style notes:** The current scaffolding files and their purple accent are to be ignored. We will establish a fresh, polished aesthetic that feels modern and highly professional. A minimal color palette emphasizing stability and trust (such as deep blues, slates, or calm greens) matched with generous whitespace and clear typography will be used. 

### Design Principles
1. **Design for Trust**: Prominently display verification badges, aggregate ratings, and transparent AI pricing estimates to reassure both customers and providers.
2. **Clarity over Clutter**: Maintain a minimal, clean aesthetic. Strip away unnecessary embellishments so the user focuses immediately on the core actions (e.g., booking, accepting a job, viewing quotes).
3. **Speed and Efficiency**: Ensure workflows for urgent dispatch requests and routine maintenance bookings have minimal friction. "Fast" is a brand pillar.
4. **Professional Neutrality**: Colors, fonts, and layout should get out of the way of the content. A reliable aesthetic is prioritized over a playful one.
